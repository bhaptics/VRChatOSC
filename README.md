# bHaptics VRChatOSC

The **bHaptics VRChat Avatar Package** applies bHaptics to your VRChat avatar, so you can feel haptic feedback in VRChat on your bHaptics devices.

Please read **[How to play VRChat with bHaptics](https://bhaptics.notion.site/How-to-play-VRChat-with-bHaptics-1226d5724b8b80229ab9e0001ab70b61)** before you start.

## Downloads
- [bHaptics VRChat Avatar Package](https://github.com/bhaptics/VRChatOSC/releases/latest/download/bHaptics-VRChatAvatar.unitypackage)
  - Check [Releases](https://github.com/bhaptics/VRChatOSC/releases) for all versions
- bHaptics Player: [PC](https://www.bhaptics.com/games/pc/software/) / [Meta Quest](https://www.meta.com/experiences/bhaptics-player/3454125161357542/)

## Quick Guide
1. **Run** bHaptics Player and **enable OSC** in VRChat.
   - How to play VRChat with bHaptics: [PC](https://bhaptics.notion.site/How-to-Play-VRChat-with-bHaptics-PC-0130853e0eaa496a8500d44546a23aca) / [Quest](https://bhaptics.notion.site/How-to-Play-VRChat-with-bHaptics-Quest-375a0f3e87ca47eb943699780cb238cd)
2. **Use** an avatar with bHaptics support.
   - Visit [bHaptics Nexus](https://vrchat.com/home/launch?worldId=wrld_8a222459-9b33-4989-8d80-57dacf35c61d)
   - Or apply bHaptics into your avatar: [PC](https://bhaptics.notion.site/How-to-Upload-an-Avatar-with-bHaptics-Devices-PC-c0479c68b8984b9d9048423b8c44f503) / [Quest](https://bhaptics.notion.site/How-to-Upload-an-Avatar-with-bHaptics-Devices-Quest-1356d5724b8b8090bae4e89cae7eb696)
